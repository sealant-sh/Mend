The dashboard groups everything by worktree: each session row leads with its worktree (branch), its
live Services hang underneath, and the detail panel is titled by the worktree. A stop shortcut lands
too — `x` (or `Shift+K`) arms against the selected worktree and a second press stops it; lowercase
`k` stays vim-up. `mend attach`/`mend rejoin` banners name the worktree as well.
